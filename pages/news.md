## Workshops

**Intro to Juggling** — this beginner workshop will teach the foundations of
ball juggling, focusing on techniques and drills to begin your juggle journey.

**Right-Left, Left-Right Passing**, with Stephane — come learn double and
triple variations while passing 6 clubs. Specifically, this workshop will
explore double, triple, and quad throws from both hands, in 4-count, 3-count
and 2-count variations, as well as how to apply it to a 3 person feeding
pattern. Want a sneak peek? Check out [this resource
guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1i61o573GKKKHux_Auuoaqke1ZYthajXeAG-Eea-mwNA/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.p60mf88ao7n5)!

**Intro to Poi**, with Dominic — learn the basics of poi spinning! In this
workshop, Dominic will be teaching the fundamental building blocks of poi
spinning. If you've never spun poi before, or are still relatively new, or just
want to review some key exercises, then this is the class for you!

**Funky Box Variations**, with Yonah — come learn some funky box variations
with juggling balls! While we won't be unpacking Pandora's box, we'll introduce
the broken and burst variations. Have fun catching and throwing under your arm
while having your brain in a twist. All levels welcome, but having a solid
shower pattern will be helpful.

**Think Inside the (Cigar) Box**, with Zeej — old prop, new possibilities.
Learn the history and basics of cigar box juggling through flips, grips, turns,
takeouts, tumbles, and tosses. We'll start with the strange physics of a single
box, build up to classic three-box manipulation, then invent tricks of our own.
By the end, beginners should understand: you don't need to memorize a hundred
cigar-box tricks. Most cigar-box juggling comes from a few simple ideas: hold →
release → turn → catch → change grip → recombine. No box experience needed, but
do bring your own boxes if you have them!

**Mixed Chocolates & Whirling Dervish Passing Patterns**, with Jed — this
workshop will explore two different multi-person passing patterns. Mixed
Chocolates is a 3 person passing pattern (with either balls or clubs) and
requires experience with chocolate bar passing plus some capability with
angles. Variations include rotating the pattern, as well as the more advanced
"Mixed Chocolate Mocha" which uses ¾ passing. Whirling Dervish is a rotating
4-count club passing pattern that can be run with 4–7 people. Variations
include rotating out the person in the middle (aka The Dervish).

## Location & Getting There

We'll be at Willard Park (2730 Hillegass Ave, Berkeley, CA 94705) — look for
the jugglers on the big grassy lawn! The park has bathrooms and water
fountains.

- ~20 minute walk from Downtown Berkeley or Ashby BART station
- Accessible via the 22, 6, 27, or 51B AC Transit bus lines, ~5 min walk
- Street parking available in the surrounding neighborhood
