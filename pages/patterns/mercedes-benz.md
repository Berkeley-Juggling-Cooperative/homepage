A 6-person pattern with 18 clubs, passing in 3-count. Three pairs stand along
the arms of a Mercedes star, which is where the pattern gets its name.

This is the same as [Berkeley Y](/patterns/berkeley-y) but with every
juggler in the inner triangle throwing backdrops.

A, C and E throw both of their passes as crossing backdrops.
