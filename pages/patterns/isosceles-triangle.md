A 3-person pattern with 10 clubs, passing in 3-count. With ten clubs between
three jugglers the pattern does not divide evenly, so the extra clubs are
carried by the 4.5 throws (doubles).

A starts half a beat after B and C.

(LR) 3 3c 3 3 4.5a 3
(LR) 3 4.5a 3 3 3b 3
