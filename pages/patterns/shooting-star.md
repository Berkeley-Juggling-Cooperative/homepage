A 4 person version of a [star](/patterns/star).

Depending on how fast you want this to be, you can do it as 2 count or 4 count and have the person with less clubs start with 0, 1, or 2 clubs.

You start in the same position as in a star, but one position of the star is left empty. The passing pattern is still the same as in the basic star pattern. That is, you always pass to the person two to your right and catch from the person two to your left (counting the empty position). The person who would pass to the empty spot is the one starting with less clubs. The person who would receive from the missing person walks to that spot when they are down to 0 (1 or 2) clubs.
