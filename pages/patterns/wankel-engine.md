# Work in progress

A 4 person takeout pattern.

3 people stand in a triangle. 4th person (M) starts next to one of the triangle persons.


```
      B


             C
A  M
```

## Start

`A`: two clubs (one each)

`M`: two clubs (flipped) (one each)

`B`, `C`: 3 clubs, 2 in their right hand, 1 in their left

## Here is what the people do during a single rotation (3 beats):

### M
Just walked (was at position `A` before), has two club and flips them (at the start, just start with two clubs flipped already). Then:

* beat 1: hand or throw your right hand club to `B`
* do a handacross
* beat 2: steal a pelf from `A` (right to left through) with empty left hand  and replace right hand club into `A`'s left hand
* do a handacross
* beat 3: steal/catch pelf/pass from `C` and step out (to the right side of `C`)

The main action is in front of `A`, a steal and a replace, whereas beat 1 is just a no-spin pass and beat 3 is just a catch. It is important to be out of the way for the `A`<->`B` pass on beat 3 and also the `B`<->`C` pass on beat 2. To achieve this, one doesn't need to walk all the way to `B` on beat 1 and just throw the club to `B` instead of handing it over and then already stand a bit closer to `C` when doing beat 2.

### A
Just walked out with 2 clubs

* beat 1: catches a club (left hand) from `B`
* beat 2: throws a right pelf that `M` steals
* gets a club replaced from `M` (where the pelf would have landed)
* beat 3: passes a right hand to 'B'

### B
Does a 2 count feed to the right/left/right

* beat 1: passes to `A`, gets a club from `M`
* beat 2: passes to `C`, receives from `C`
* beat 3: passes to `A`, receives from `A`

### C
Only one pass on beat 2

* beat 1: right self
* beat 2: right pass to `B`, receives from `B`
* beat 3: pelf that `M` catches.  Easier for `M` if thrown somewhat into the middle of the pattern.


## Rotation

M -> A -> B -> C

## Full cycle for one person:

Starting with `M` walking in and the names as arranged in the diagram above.

* `M` walks halfway towards B with 2 clubs (flipped)
* throw right hand club to `B`'s left hand, handacross, turn towards `A`
* steal/replace pelf from `A`, turn towards `C`
* grep pelf/pass from `C`, step out with 2 clubs and turn facing `A`
* catch pass to `A` -> 3 clubs again
* right pelf and getting a club replaces from new `M`
* pass to `A` (start of a 4 pass 2-count sequence)
* pass to `B`, pass to `A`, pass to `B`
* self, pass to `C` (now taking the place of `B`), through pelf towards the middle for the new `M` (original `B`) to catch
* flip remaining two clubs and walk halfway towards the middle
