# Work in progress

A 4 person takeout pattern.

3 people stand in a triangle. 4th person starts next to one of the triangle persons.


```
      B


             C
A  M
```


Just before swap:

person in the middle has one club, greps pelf and walks out
person on the outside has then only 2 clubs, flips them and walks in


When new M walks in:

M walks towards B, when M is flipping, B is passing to A, M is handing to B (as incoming pass)

M is then turning to A and does an exchange, B is passing to C (2 count)

A does a pelf/pass to M and M replaces on to the right hand of A

B is then passing to A again

C after passing to A, does a pelf to M and then M and C switch

Here is the full cycle for a single person:

when as old M the person just walked out:

has 2 clubs, gets on from the left, does an pass to new M and gets one from M, then passes to the left again, then pass to right (and gets one from new M), then self (and gets from left), then right, the pelf and back to being M
