The normal Y variation (you always pass and receive from the same people), but with only 3 people.

Two people start normal with 3 clubs (2 clubs in their right hand). One person starts with with 0 clubs (or 1 or 2 if you want to walk earlier and make the pattern faster).

One position of the Y is left empty. The person who would pass to the empty spot is the one starting with less clubs. The person who would receive from the missing person walks to that spot when they are down to 0 (1 or 2) clubs.
