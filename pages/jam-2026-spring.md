# Berkeley Jam Spring Fling 2026

Another community-led juggling and circus arts gathering in the park — bring
your props, or borrow ours, and fling, toss and spin them around.

**What**: Completely free juggling and circus arts jam

**When**: Sunday 12 April 2026, 11am–5ish

**Where**: UC Berkeley campus, west side of the new Undergraduate Academic
Building (also the south-side entrance of the Valley Life Sciences building)

**Who**: All ages and skill levels welcome

Heavy rain on the Saturday moved the jam from Willard Park to a covered campus
location — still an outdoor event, just with a roof. It went ahead regardless,
and despite the weather there was a fantastic turnout, with workshops, passing
sessions and community juggling throughout the day.
