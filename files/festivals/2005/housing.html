Housing and Accomodations

Members of the Berkeley juggling community have generously offered "crash-space" (either indoor or outdoor) for attendees traveling to Berkeley for the Festival. There will be room for everyone who wants housing, but they'd prefer advance notice: if you'd like to take them up on that hospitality, please contact us. Those looking for slightly more luxurious accomodations than floor space might be interesed in one of the following hotels:

  • The French Hotel
  • Super 8 Motel
  • YMCA

Camping
