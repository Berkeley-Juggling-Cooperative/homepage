3rd Annual Berkeley Juggling & Unicycling Festival

October 5 - 7th, 2007 :: King Middle School, Berkeley, CA

Variety Show

What: Berkeley Juggling and Unicycle Festival Public Show
When: Saturday 10/6, 7:30 PM
Where: King Middle School theater
How Much: $15 for adults, $8 for children under 12

Last year, we put on a fantastic public show replete with local talent, and this year we're looking to out do that effort. We've put together an amazing cast of local performers and some visiting pros. Names like Vova Galchenko, Ryan Woessner, Ashley Foster, Matt Hall, and many others.

The Variety Show will be family friendly and open to all ages. All show proceeds are put towards the (significant) costs of hosting the Berkeley Juggling and Unicycle Festival. Aside from showing up with a smile on your face, attending the Public Show is the single best thing you can do to support this festival and ensure that we can all come back, year after year.

Would you like to perform in the Public Show? We're looking for a number of small, simple (read: no stage setup) acts to fill the gaps between performers. If you're interested, contact us or just talk to us at the registration table on Saturday to sign up!

brought to you by the Berkeley Juggling Cooperative
