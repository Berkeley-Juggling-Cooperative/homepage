Map and directions

BART

The Berkeley Juggling Festival is easily accessed from the North Berkeley BART station and we want to encourage attendees to use public transit. From the North Berkeley station, simply exit onto Sacramento, turn left and follow the driving directions outlined below:

AC Transit

The #9 bus line, from west Berkeley or downtown, travels right past King School on Hopkins. The 7 and 18 lines stop a few blocks away at Shattuck and Rose, and the 79 runs along MLK and stops at Rose.

Driving Directions Festival

  1. Take Hwy. 80 to Berkeley from either the north or south
  2. Exit University Ave. eastbound (toward the Hills, not the Bay)
  3. Turn left at the fourth stoplight onto Sacramento Rd.
  4. Drive north 10 blocks on Sacramento, then turn right onto Rose Ave.
  5. King Middle School is 3 blocks east on the left-hand side of Rose Ave.
  6. Note: Please pull off the street and park on the asphalt behind the school proper! We need to be very sensitive that the area surrounding King MS is a residential one.
  7. If you have trouble finding parking, consider parking at the nearby North Berkeley BART station (see map)
