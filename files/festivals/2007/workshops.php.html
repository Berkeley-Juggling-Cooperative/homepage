3rd Annual Berkeley Juggling & Unicycling Festival

October 5 - 7th, 2007 :: King Middle School, Berkeley, CA

Schedule and Workshops

The final workshop schedule will probably be available only shortly before the festival. Nevertheless, below is a list of some workshops that will take place.

In case you want to give a workshop, please contact us (disabled since festival is over).

Unicycle workshop schedule (subject to change)

  • Friday
    • 6pm - Unicycle repair workshop (Tom Holub)
    • 7pm - Unicycle basketball demo game (in gym) (Berkeley unicyclists)
  • Saturday
    • Learn to ride, informal help and extra unicycles available all day
    • 10am - Big-wheel rolling party to the Berkeley Marina
    • 1pm - Mountain Unicycling (MUni) basics (Tom Holub)
    • 2pm - My First MUni Ride, Tilden Park (carpool/shuttle)
    • 4pm - Unicycle games : Bar race (beginner-friendly), Spoon race, Tug-Of-War, Flag Tag
    • 5pm - Freestyle expert skills demonstration/workshop (Ryan Woessner, world freestyle and pairs freestyle champion)
    • 7:30pm - Variety Show, King School auditorium
  • Sunday
    • Learn to ride, informal help and extra unicycles available all day
    • 10am Mountain unicycle (MUni) ride, Joaquin Miller Park (carpool/shuttle)
    • 2pm - Unicycling skills and toys (John Foss, President Emeritus, Unicycling Society of America)
    • 3pm - Unicycle ultimate frisbee
