Big Wheel Party (Berkeley bike routes and waterfront paths, Sunday October 7th)

If MUni and basketball sound like too much work for you, come join us for a leisurely group party on flat roads and paths along the Berkeley waterfront. There will be a thumping sound system riding along with us to get everyone in the party mood. We would recommend at least a 29" unicycle, but this is not a race, so feel free to ride whatever you have. There may be some extra big wheel unis around; come by a little early, and if you're lucky, you'll get to try out someone's Coker (36" wheel) or a geared unicycle!

The entire ride is 13km (8 miles), and it will probably take about two hours. There's a shorter option for people who have smaller wheels or who just don't want to do that much distance; you can meet us at the Sea Breeze Market and Deli, just on the west side of the freeway, and loop around the Berkeley Marina from there.

Directions:
  • Go west on Rose three blocks to California; turn left.
  • Take California .5 miles, across University to Addison; loop around the circle a few times and turn right.
  • Take Addison west to where it ends at Browning (.5 miles); bear left.
  • Take your first right off Browning (Allston).
  • Take Allston across San Pablo Ave to 10th Street; turn right.
  • Take your first left off 10th (Addison).
  • Take Addison .5 miles to where it ends at Aquatic Park; turn right onto the bike path.
  • Take the bike path up and over the freeway (nice views of the Bay and San Francisco); turn left at the end.
  • Pass the Sea Breeze Market on your left
  • Take your first left on University.
  • Take your first right on Marina.
  • Where Marina ends at Spinnaker, bear right onto the bike path and go straight.
  • Take the bike path around the border of Cesar Chavez Park (2 miles).
  • Reverse directions to return.
