We’ll be JAMming in the meadow at Joaquin Miller Park. From Joaquin Miller Road, go to the main park entrance (Sanborn Drive); the meadow is just inside the park to the right.

The AC Transit 339 bus from Fruitvale BART stops at the park entrance.

The parking closest to the meadow is outside the entrance on Joaquin Miller Road. The parking lot inside the park by the community center will be reserved for a private event in the afternoon. There is another, larger parking lot inside the park, a bit of a walk from the meadow.
