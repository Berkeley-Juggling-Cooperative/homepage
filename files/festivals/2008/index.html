Unicycle Rides & Games

It is the Berkeley Juggling and Unicycling Festival after all... Our festival always features a variety of unicycle rides. From the distance ride (complete with Critical Mass sound system!) to muni rides and trials sessions, we feature something for every rider. The weekend also plays host to several unicycle basketball games and our internationally reknowned tournament of champions featuring events like flag uni, tug-of-war and Marc's Malicious Obstacle Course of Doom... ok, maybe it's not that bad.

Construction Zone

The Berkeley Juggling Cooperative firmly believes everyone can learn to juggle. The construction zone is the perfect place for new jugglers to pick up the basics. In addition to an assortment of basic props, the Construction Zone features friendly volunteers excited to spread the joys of juggling.

Jugglers: do you want to make someone's day? Why not spend an hour teaching newbies in the construction zone? All we can offer for your efforts is total elightenment and our sincere thanks, but hey, that's something. Sign up at the front table.
