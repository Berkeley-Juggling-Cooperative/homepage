All the workshops in this huge list are absolutely free! Come learn some new skills; whatever your level, there’s a workshop for you.

    • 3:00 pm: Contact Juggling Fundamentals (3:00 pm–4:00 pm · with Richard Hartnell)Contact juggling has one of the least popular learning curves in all the circus arts. Besides lacking a lot of easy tricks that can be picked up early in one's journey, contact juggling also encourages a lot of bad habits in self-study that can make a new contact juggler plateau forever. This workshop includes all the necessary knowledge to skip all those bad habits and cut months off the journey of the basic or intermediate contact juggler.

      http://www.gravitydefiance.net
      Richard Hartnell is one of the USA's eminent contact jugglers. A co-founder of the Bellingham Circus Guild and five-year resident artist at Oakland's Vulcan Studios, he now performs professionally all over the USA and Europe. Most recently, Richard has just returned from a 26-day performance stint at the Edinburgh Festival Fringe and was drafted into the fire gala at the European Juggling Convention, after which he was commended by Michael Moschen on the quality of his performance. Richard considers performance a kind of activism, and tries to help enable other manipulators to positively change the world at every turn.

For those with some contact juggling experience, here's a workshop to get you off the second big plateau in contact juggling: the elbows. In this class we'll deal with chestrolls, folding lines, bridge rolls, rolls behind and around the neck, head rolls, and other goods. The goal: to build a framework for bodyrolling that allows all your rolls to connect to one another and build some super-long, satisfying, beautiful bodyrolling sequences. If we have time then we'll also explore some in-depth isolation techniques.
      Intermediate / Advanced level class.
