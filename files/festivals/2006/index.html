2nd Annual Berkeley Juggling and Unicycling Festival 2006

4pm Fri, Sept 29th 2006 – 5pm Sunday Oct 1st 2006
King Middle School, 1781 Rose Street, Berkeley, CA
Free admission! Free Workshops!

Jam packed schedule every day includes:

Friday night
The real Renegade show - not to be missed! Hosted by comedian Frank Olivier and Renegade Juggling with an incredible cast of performers!
Saturday
Full day of workshops by the Berkeley Juggling Coop, Stanford Juggling Research Institute, and advanced juggling techniques with Jeff Napier among many others. Also for the one-wheelers there will be Mountain Unicycle rides and a Unicycle Basketball tournament (team registration Friday).
Saturday night
Variety show with Frank Olivier, Scot Nery, Matt Hall, Kaori Matsuzawa, and many others!
Sunday
Massive prop swap hosted by San Francisco Circus Arts Center! Tons of circus, juggling, unicycling, clowning, and other props for sale, trade, or barter.
Everyday
Juggling and unicycling games (ie tug-of-war on 1 wheel!), lots of workshops taught by experts, juggling and unicycling basics, and if you are in the market for quality new props, Renegade Juggling, Serious Juggling and Neon Husky will be there selling their wares.

See the pictures from last year's festival to get an idea of what it's like.

For more information, email us at festival.info@berkeleyjuggling.org.
