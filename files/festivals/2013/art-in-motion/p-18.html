Art In Motion: A circus arts show benefitting the Arts

The Berkeley Juggling Collective is proud to present “Art In Motion: A circus arts show benefitting Arts in Education and medicine.” Emceed by the legendary Jeff Raz (Pickle Family Circus, Cirque du Soleil), this event will showcase incredible circus talent. Saturday, October 5, 7:30 PM, Kofman Auditorium, 2200 Central, Alameda.

Come be amused and amazed by talent from:
Cirque Du Soleil,
Ringling Bros.,
Pickle Family Circus,
America’s Got Talent,
and more!

Proceeds benefit arts education in Alameda and the Medical Clown Project.

Part of the Bay Area Circus Arts Festival
